$$d_u(t)\ge d_u(t_0)-|u|\,|t-t_0|\ge \frac{1}{n+1},$$

$$|t-t_0|\le h_u:=\frac{d_u(t_0)-\frac{1}{n+1}}{|u|}.$$

$$H:=\min_{u\in V}h_u.$$

$$h_u\ge \frac{\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}}{|u|}=\frac{1}{n(n+1)|u|}\ge \frac{1}{n(n+1)M}=h.$$
