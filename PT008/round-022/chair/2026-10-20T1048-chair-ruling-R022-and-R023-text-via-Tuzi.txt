一、Argon 的反例：击中选择器，没有击中数学存在性

取：

V=(1,2,9,30).

Argon 报告现有的局外人轮换和限定后备规则未能为它选出成功时间。

不过，直接选择：

t=\frac{41}{150}

四个距离是：

\frac{41}{150},\quad
\frac{34}{75},\quad
\frac{23}{50},\quad
\frac15.

全部安全。

甚至更简单的：

\boxed{t=\frac14}

也成功，因为距离为：

\boxed{\frac14,\frac12,\frac14,\frac12}.

因此，这组速度并不存在安全时间困难。

困难在于规则没能选择到已有的安全时间。

这正是它作为反例的价值。

必须保留 Hesper 的限定：Argon 的检查针对某套具体选择器，尚不足以证明所有可能的 LS-R、CLR v2 实施方式都失败。特别是 CLR 允许改变参考形状、相位格及余数表时，不能只检查某一张表就宣称排除整个定理族。

二、Opus 的结构核：有意义，但名称不能误导

Opus 提出了结构核 ，并观察某些速度组合可能让保留旧门槛的归纳策略失效。

这里有一个重要区别：

结构核不是「没有安全时间的集合」。

一般五跑者的安全时间存在性早已得到证明。

结构核真正应该描述的是：

对于某一类明确规定的归纳传递规则，是否存在无法保留其所需安全信息的速度组合？

例如：

(1,3,4,7).

在五跑者门槛 下， 成功，四个距离分别为：

\frac15,\frac25,\frac15,\frac25.

但此时速度 1 位于安全区下沿，速度 4 位于上沿。

向左调整会破坏前者；向右调整会破坏后者。

所以 是一个局部孤立的成功时间。

这说明：

一个成功时间点，未必能提供可供下一轮使用的左右安全窗口。

但它不能证明其他时间选择、其他阈值或其他归纳数据也必然失败。

因此 Opus 的 CORE、TF 和引理 N 应继续接受严格审阅，尤其必须写清楚 的定义和量词。

三、我希望改变的研究重点

过去几轮，我们不断尝试：

\text{一个成功时间}
\longrightarrow
\text{加入下一位跑者}.

但一个成功时间可能是孤立点。

即使保留一个正长度窗口，加入新速度后，它也可能被切成多个互不相连的小窗口。

所以我们需要考虑更丰富的归纳对象。

设旧速度集为 ，门槛为 ，定义完整安全集：

S_\delta(V)=
\left\{
t>0:\min_{v\in V}\|vt\|\ge\delta
\right\}.

我们不一定要把整个集合逐点存下来。

更值得寻找的是一个有限且可计算的证书（Finite Certificate），例如：

\mathcal I_n(V)=
\bigl(\text{安全相位族、间隔界、覆盖规则}\bigr).

它必须满足两个条件：

第一，能够从证书直接算出安全时间。

第二，加入新速度后，能够通过确定性操作再生下一层证书。

注意，仅仅证明安全时间族存在，不等于证明这种证书能被有限地计算和传递。

这才是 R023 应该检验的核心问题。

Chair’s R022 Ruling

R022 PARTIAL.

All four seats identify the same unresolved gap: the current constructions do not automatically preserve a controlled-gap family of safe times under the addition of another runner.

Opus’s N, TF, CORE, and revised H remain PROOF CANDIDATES.

FO and TL have meaningful non-author verification but remain conditional constructions. OB must be restricted to its precisely defined transfer mechanisms.

Argon’s V=(1,2,9,30) is a valid counterexample to the tested selection procedure, not a counterexample to the existence of safe times.

General F5 and F_n remain UNFINISHED.

North Star

The original goal is a deterministic construction F_n for arbitrary nonzero relative speeds, from two to forty-seven runners and beyond.

Do not replace this goal with a catalogue of special F5 cases.

A universal existence theorem is not itself the requested explicit time formula.

Central Question

Can we define finite constructive data I_n(V) from which:

1. A successful time is obtained directly;
2. The data for n+1 runners can be computed from I_n(V) and the new speed;
3. The defining guarantees are preserved at the new safety threshold?

A controlled-gap safe-time family is one candidate. It is not assumed sufficient.

Track A — Opus: Define the Invariant

Give a precise mathematical definition of a candidate I_n(V).

Specify its finite representation, the operations allowed, the bound on its size or complexity, and how it produces a safe time.

Determine whether PS_n, PS*, APS, TL or the structural core C_n can be expressed in this framework.

Review and revise the formal statements N, TF and CORE.

Pay particular attention to the order of quantifiers in CORE:

* For every selection rule F?
* For every outsider?
* For every permitted phase lattice?
* Or only a fixed, finite family of rules?

Prove only the strongest statement actually supported.

Track B — Qwen: Alternative Safe-Time Families

Start from Q-GATE and the exact fixed-phase characterization Q-FSR.

Investigate whether retaining several safe times, rather than a single time, helps insert the outsider.

Propose a finite family that can be generated without unbounded searching.

Test the family on both:

V=(1,3,4,7)

and

V=(1,2,9,30).

Explain whether a new runner can destroy every member of the family.

Do not assume the safe-time set is periodic for arbitrary irrational real speeds.

Track C — Argon: One Rigorous Obstruction

Choose one fully specified proposed invariant or regeneration rule.

Construct a counterexample to the stated universal claim if possible.

Distinguish:

* Failure of a particular selector;
* Failure of every selector in a specified finite class;
* Failure of a proposed mathematical invariant;
* Nonexistence of any explicit formula.

Only the first three are appropriate conclusions from restricted counterexamples.

One complete hand-checkable example is preferred over a large numerical sweep.

Track D — DeepSeek: Conditional Regeneration Lemma

Formulate and prove the strongest valid conditional lemma for passing a safe-time family from n to n+1 runners.

The output must contain:

1. Exact input certificate;
2. Exact safety threshold;
3. Explicit time-selection rule;
4. Proof the required time exists;
5. Explicit spacing or complexity bound;
6. Conditions needed for regeneration;
7. A determination whether these conditions are automatically preserved.

Review the revised H1–H3 and the CORE quantifiers independently.

For leftward moves, retain the strict positive-time requirement.

Common Test Cases

V₁=(1,3,4,7): a case with isolated successful times.

V₂=(1,2,9,30): the tested outsider selector fails, although t=1/4 succeeds.

V₃=(1,30,60,80): a non-first-cluster example.

The purpose is to test structural claims, not rediscover the known safe times.
