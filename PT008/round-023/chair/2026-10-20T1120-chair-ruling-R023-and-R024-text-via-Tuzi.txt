North Star

For arbitrary input speeds and runner count n, construct a deterministic safe time F_n, using a uniform mechanism from 2 to 47 runners and beyond.

A finite data structure is not enough unless it yields a guaranteed safe time.

The Central Distinction

Separate three mathematical claims:

(A) REPRESENTATION:
The safety set at a specified threshold can be represented effectively.

(B) UPDATE:
After adding a speed and lowering the threshold, the representation can be updated.

(C) GUARANTEE:
The updated safety set is nonempty, and a successful time can be selected by a proven finite operation.

For unrestricted real speeds, (C) is the unresolved target. Do not infer it from (A) or (B).

Track A — Opus: A Guaranteed Certificate?

Formalize SPC for commensurable rational speeds.

Give exact common-period requirements, breakpoint counts and update complexity.

Investigate whether an enriched certificate—interval distribution, covering radius, or controlled gaps—can imply the next-layer nonemptiness condition.

Find a counterexample if the proposed certificate is insufficient.

Do not assume a finite global period for irrational real speed ratios.

Track B — Qwen: Interval Regeneration

Use the corrected interval-certificate version.

State exact endpoint formulas when a new speed is added.

Study whether keeping all maximal safe intervals, rather than one selected interval, helps guarantee at least one surviving interval.

Test inputs with isolated points and with widely separated speed scales.

Find the exact additional condition that would force the updated intersection to be nonempty.

Do not confuse a finite calculation under rational commensurability with a general real-speed proof.

Track C — Argon: One Adversarial Obstruction

Choose one precise nonemptiness guarantee proposed by Track A or B.

Provide one hand-verifiable counterexample if it fails.

Check all relevant outsider choices when the claim quantifies over all of them.

Reconcile the answer block with the complete proof before submission.

A short, correct counterexample is preferred over large computations.

Track D — DeepSeek: Regenerative Guarantee Lemma

Start from CRL.

* Availability of its hypotheses;
