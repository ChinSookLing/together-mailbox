### §3.1 新定理：锚 $b{+}c$，$\tau = 2$（PROOF CANDIDATE）

**定理 Q-B2.** 设 $0 < a < b < c < d$，且

$$\frac{3}{2} \le \frac{c}{b} \le 9, \tag{H2}$$
$$\frac{b+c}{a} \le 10, \tag{H3}$$
$$\frac{3}{5} \le \frac{d}{b+c} \le \frac{9}{10}. \tag{H4}$$

则 $t = \dfrac{2}{b+c} > 0$ 满足 $\|at\|,\;\|bt\|,\;\|ct\|,\;\|dt\| \ge \dfrac{1}{5}$。

**证明。** 记 $S = b + c$，$\theta = b/S \in (0, 1/2)$。$t = 2/S$。

**(i) $b,c$ 条（配对）：** $bt + ct = 2b/S + 2c/S = 2 \in \mathbb{Z}$。故 $\|ct\| = \|bt\|$。

$bt = 2\theta$。由 (H2)：$c/b \ge 3/2 \implies \theta = b/(b+c) \le b/(b + 3b/2) = 2/5$。$c/b \le 9 \implies \theta \ge b/(b+9b) = 1/10$。

故 $\theta \in [1/10,\, 2/5]$，$2\theta \in [1/5,\, 4/5]$。$\operatorname{frac}(2\theta) = 2\theta$（因 $2\theta < 1$）。$\|bt\| = \min(2\theta, 1-2\theta) \ge 1/5$。✓

**(ii) $a$ 条：** $at = 2a/S = 2/s$，$s = S/a$。由 (H3)：$s \le 10$，$at \ge 2/10 = 1/5$。由 $a < b$：$at < 2b/S = 2\theta \le 4/5$。故 $at \in [1/5, 4/5)$。$\|at\| \ge 1/5$。✓

**(iii) $d$ 条：** $dt = 2d/S = 2w$，$w = d/S$。由 (H4)：$w \in [3/5, 9/10]$。$2w \in [6/5, 9/5]$。

$\operatorname{frac}(2w) = 2w - 1 \in [1/5, 4/5]$。$\|dt\| = \min(2w-1,\, 2-2w) \ge \min(1/5, 1/5) = 1/5$。✓

$t = 2/S > 0$。$\square$
