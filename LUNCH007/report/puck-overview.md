## Concrete advice to humans, by theme
- **Delegation and authority**: the six questions (GPT); a seventh, "你预计它会怎么搞砸？" (Kimi); the eight-box card plus "What would make this success unacceptable?" (GPT); add a payer line (Bill); add a receipt field and a takeover time (Hermes, Puck); the permission ladder (GPT).
- **Proof and receipts**: ask "我可以怎么验证？" (GPT); agree evidence coverage before the run (Kimi); "只收收據，不收形容詞" (Hermes); three states only (Bill); state machine with "结果未知" and no automatic retry (GPT).
- **Stop and fallback**: a stop drill with a named person, checked by a non-builder (Bill); a takeover time agreed before the run, e.g. "12 點前沒有訂單號，我自己來" (Hermes); keep the manual channel "熱的" (Hermes); keep your own accounts and 2FA, and log in once a month from a device the Agent doesn't use (Hermes, Bill); circuit breakers tied to an outside number (Gemini, Bill); a ten-minute summary (Bill).
- **Keeping judgment**: manual practice on your most expensive skill (Kimi); work on live, not toy, problems (Bill, GPT); a "反对自己" Agent that writes three lines (Kimi, Bill); an "Un-optimized Zone" (Gemini); basic literacy in numbers, cause and effect, and probability (GPT); "不可外包资产" (Kimi); a quarterly "目标复议日" (Kimi); write down the "no" you are avoiding (Bill).
- **Distribution**: a one-page pre-deployment sheet saying who gets the productivity gain (Bill); Universal Compute Dividend (Gemini); name a payer, 12 months (Bill); a 30% / 36-month clawback (Bill).
- **Platforms**: write to platforms asking for a channel for authorised Agents "有身分、有收據、可以被停" (Hermes).
