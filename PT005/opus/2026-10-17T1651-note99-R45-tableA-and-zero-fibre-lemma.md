## 2. The zero-fibre lemma: PROVED (chair), p-free
**Setting:** level M = 2ᵏp, k ≥ 1. A coordinate w = 2ᵛu (u odd) has depth d = k − v if v < k, and depth 0 if v ≥ k. Look at the **zero fibre**: odd multiples T = p·t (t odd) with T ≤ M/2, i.e. t odd in 1..2^(k−1).

> **Lemma Z.** At level M:
> - (a) a coordinate of depth 0 covers every T in the zero fibre;
> - (b) a coordinate of depth 1, 2, 3 or 4 covers none of them;
> - (c) a coordinate of depth d ≥ 5 covers exactly 1/8 of them.
>
> **Hence:** if a node at level M is improper, then F0 ≥ 1 or F5 ≥ 8.

**Proof.**
1. T·w = p·t·2ᵛu, so ‖T·w‖_M = p·‖2ᵛ·tu‖_{2ᵏ}. The cover condition 16·‖Tw‖_M < M becomes ‖2ᵛ·m‖_{2ᵏ} < 2^(k−4), with m = tu odd.
2. **v ≥ k:** the left side is 0, so T is covered. This is (a).
3. **v < k:** ‖2ᵛm‖_{2ᵏ} = 2ᵛ·‖m‖_{2^d} with d = k − v. The condition is ‖m‖_{2^d} < 2^(d−4).
   - If d ≤ 4, the right side is ≤ 1, but m is odd, so ‖m‖ ≥ 1. No T is covered. This is (b).
   - If d ≥ 5, the odd residues r mod 2^d with min(r, 2^d − r) < 2^(d−4) number 2·2^(d−5) = 2^(d−4), out of 2^(d−1) odd residues: a 1/8 share.
4. **The share is exact.** As t runs over the odd numbers in 1..2^(k−1), m = tu runs over the odd residues mod 2^d evenly when d ≤ k − 1. When d = k (v = 0), it runs over half of them, but the condition is symmetric under r ↔ 2^d − r, so the share is still exactly 1/8. This is (c).
5. **Corollary.** Every T in the zero fibre must be covered. With no depth-0 coordinate, only depth ≥ 5 coordinates help, each with a 1/8 share, so at least 8 of them are needed. ∎

**Checks.**
- Matches Puck's and Hesper's exact counts (d0f0d08), indexed one level up: their "depth ≥ 4 at the parent" is depth ≥ 5 here.
- Chair numeric spot check at p = 19/23/29/61, k = 3..8: every case agrees.
- **Explains §1.3:** an open child at 2M needs a depth-0 coordinate at 2M, or F5 ≥ 8. A depth-0 coordinate at 2M must come from a depth-0 coordinate at M with the right lift bit. So F0 = 0 at M kills all children unless F4(M) ≥ 8.

**Status:** PROVED by the chair, short. CHECKED numerically by three programs. DeepSeek to read it line by line (R45).

**Why it matters:**
- The zero fibre is now completely understood, and **the same for every prime**.
- It costs every surviving branch one forced lift bit per level: some depth-0 coordinate must stay at depth 0.
- If the other fibres ±a (mod p) can be understood the same way, the death of a branch becomes a counting problem on 2-adic digits. That would be the first statement here that could carry over to 17, 18 and 20 runners.
