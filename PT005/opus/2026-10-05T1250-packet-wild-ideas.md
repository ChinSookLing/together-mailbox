THE PROBLEM IN SIX LINES
Lonely runner conjecture for 16 runners: for any 15 distinct positive integer speeds there is a time t with every
||v_i t|| ≥ 1/16. Known proof method (Allikvere, arXiv:2609.02604 v2): a counterexample's speeds have product
< e^494.56 (proved on our table, ledger L1, L8); for each prime p a computer "gate" shows p divides the product;
enough gates (Σ log p > 481.07) give a contradiction. Large primes (p ≥ 239) close cheaply (p = 239 closed,
reproduced 3×). The cost is about 74,000 CPU-hours if we use only large primes up to ~733.

different cores gave EXACTLY 496,000 improper lifts. Different cores, identical count. Why? (A picture of the
covering, time classes in discrete-log order, shows every speed covers a shifted copy of the same shape.)
