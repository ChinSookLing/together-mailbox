Q1 — THE CONVERSE

(0) FACT — the census needs a filter before Q1 is well-posed. By the forced-family lemma, any lift whose zero-classes dominate is improper regardless of its free coordinates: a core row lifted with 14 zero-coordinates + 1 odd, or 15 zero-coordinates, or 13 zero + (odd, ≡8-mod-16) free pair, is improper and primitive, yet is not of the "13 zero + 2 odd" form. So "improper" in the census must mean "survivor of L1–L6" or "primitive and non-descending and 13-zero-parameterised". This matters because the census claim then decomposes into (i) no improper lift with z ≤ 12 zero-coordinates — the real content, and open — and (ii) a filter statement about z ≥ 13. I answer both readings.

(1) FACT — exact blocked-lift classification (I use the chair's hint (i) and sharpen "at most" to exact counts with parity structure; this sharpening is where all content lives). Fix a class τ with representative t ∈ [1, p−1]; its 16 lifts are t_j = t + jp, j ∈ Z_16. For a coordinate u write ut mod 16p = c + p·s with c = ut mod p ∈ [1, p−1], s ∈ Z_16. Then position(u, t_j) = c + p·(s + uj mod 16), which is bad iff s + uj ≡ 0 or 15 (mod 16) (positions c and c+15p; distance-exactly-p positions never occur since c ∈ [1,p−1]). Hence, with v = v₂(u):
- v = 0: blocks exactly {−su⁻¹, (15−s)u⁻¹} — exactly 2 lifts of EVERY class, one even j, one odd j;
- v = 1: exactly {j₀, j₀+8} — exactly 2 lifts of every class, same parity;
- v = 2: ∅ or {j₀, j₀+4, j₀+8, j₀+12} — 4 lifts, one parity;
- v = 3: ∅ or one full parity class of 8 j's. With u = 8y and ρ = y·t mod 2p: even j's ⟺ ρ ∈ [0, p/8) ∪ [15p/8, 2p); odd j's ⟺ ρ ∈ [7p/8, 9p/8).
So odd and v=1 coordinates reach every class with weight exactly 2; v=2, v=3 coordinates reach only window classes, ≈ (p−1)/16 ≈ 14 classes per window at p = 223. Astra 23's ledger S₁₆ = 2a+2b+4c+8d is confirmed; the parity/window refinement is new.

(2) FACT — reduction theorem (the part I can prove). Let z = #coordinates ≡ 0 (mod 16), f = 15 − z.
- z = 15: f = 0, a non-dominated class has all 16 lifts good.
- z = 14: f = 1, capacity ≤ 8 < 16.
- z = 13: capacity ≤ 8+8 = 16, with equality only for a free pair both ≡ 8 (mod 16), taking complementary parities at every non-dominated class. Any other pair, in particular any pair containing an odd coordinate (2+8 = 10 < 16), fails. Note that the (8 mod 16, 8 mod 16) exception forces gcd ≥ 8: the row descends to level 2p and is not a level-16 object.
Therefore, under the primitive (non-descending) reading: every improper lift with z ≥ 13 has dominating zero-coordinates — proved — and the converse is exactly equivalent to: no improper lift with z ≤ 12.

(3) FACT — conditional counterexamples (explicit lifts, per the chair's invitation).
(3a) Literal reading. Let A be a 13-set with S − A = Z_N ∖ {τ₀} (unique miss). Take the row A ∪ {b₁, b₂}; lift A at its zero-lifts; lift bᵢ at uᵢ = 8yᵢ with y₁t₀ mod 2p in the even-window and y₂t₀ mod 2p in the odd-window (uᵢ ≡ 8 mod 16 is the pinned lift of class bᵢ = 8yᵢ mod p; this is the {8ε, 8(p+2j)} family — ε = ±1, j ∈ Z parametrise odd y). Every class τ ≠ τ₀ is blocked by a zero-coordinate at all 16 lifts; τ₀'s even j's are blocked by u₁, odd j's by u₂. Improper, zeros do not dominate; gcd = 8 (descends). For fixed τ₀ there are ≈ p/8 ≈ 28 admissible y's per window at p = 223, against ≈ 27 forbidden (A's classes, y ≡ 0 mod p), so a clean pair exists unless A is window-adversarial.
(3b) Primitive reading. Let C be a core, a ∈ C with private set priv_C(a) = {τ₀} (private = covered by a alone). Then A = C − a is a 12-set with M(A) = priv(a) = {τ₀} exactly. Row = C ∪ {b₁, b₂}; zero-lift A's 12 classes; lift b₁, b₂ as u₁, u₂ ≡ 8 (mod 16) in the two windows; lift a itself at any odd lift. z = 12, f = 3, primitive (one odd coordinate), improper by the same per-class check, zeros non-dominating — and the row contains the core C, so the witness lives inside the census's own enumeration space. Conversely (FACT): every 12-set with a unique miss extends to a core by any σ ∈ (S − τ₀) ∖ A (nonempty: 13 candidates vs |A| = 12), and priv(σ) = {τ₀}; so unique-miss 12-sets are in exact correspondence with private-1 vertices of cores, and the private-set scan below is exhaustive for them. With |M| = 2 one needs both missed classes in E(u₁) ∩ O(u₂) (≈ 13 free pairs — borderline); |M| ≥ 3 is dead for the z = 12 route. The z ≤ 11 routes need v₂/v₃ mixes (e.g. z = 11 needs ≥ 2 coords ≡ 8 mod 16, or one such + two ≡ 4 mod 8) with 3+ window coincidences per missed class — strictly more rigid.

(4) ESTIMATE — abundance. γ = 13 forces every core to be irredundant (empty private set would give a 12-dominator), so private sizes are ≥ 1; in arc-circulants of this type I expect private sizes 1–4 with mass at 1–2, giving ~10¹–10³ private-1 vertices (hence unique-miss 12-sets) and a similar or larger count of unique-miss 13-sets (one swap below domination) at p = 223. Prediction: the literal converse is false at 223; the primitive converse hinges on the private-set census. Note the stakes: if a witness exists, the 223 census has a hole (it claims no improper lift outside 13+2odd); if none exists, we have discovered a lemma — "every core of Γ₂₂₃ is 2-irredundant" — and the census's z ≤ 12 completeness is explained rather than assumed. Both outcomes are progress; the test below decides which.

Q2 — 17 RUNNERS

(1) FACT — flat weights. At level 17p the same computation gives positions c + p(s + uj mod 17) and bad σ ∈ {0, 16}; every coordinate ≢ 0 (mod 17) blocks exactly 2 of the 17 lifts of every class. There is no 2-adic ladder because 17 is prime.

(2) FACT — the correct chain. A non-dominated class needs ⌈17/2⌉ = 9 free coordinates (8×2 = 16 < 17). So any improper level-17 lift with z ≥ 8 has dominating zeros; and the counting bound γ(Γ′) ≥ N/|S′| = (p−1)/(2⌊p/17⌋) > 8 holds for every prime p > 17, i.e. γ ≥ 9 for free. Hence at every 17-door: improper ⟹ z ≥ γ (forced family) or z ≤ 7; the zone 8 ≤ z < γ is empty. "γ = K−2 ⇒ only forced families" is the wrong analogue: at 16 runners the forcing came from the weight-8 exception plus primitivity (γ = 13 = 15−2 was a coincidence of that), and at 17 runners no coordinate covers more than 2 of 17 lifts, so the forcing threshold is ⌈17/2⌉ = 9, always satisfied. At 223 itself γ′ = 13 < 14 = K−2 and the gate logic still works (forced families are 13 zeros + 3 free, total free weight 6 < 17) — a live counterexample to the necessity of the K−2 shape.

(3) FACT — free doors. Γ′(p) = Γ(p) whenever ⌊p/16⌋ = ⌊p/17⌋: at p = 103, 107, 109, 127, 137, 139, 157, 173, 191, 223, 239 the 17-runner graph is the 16-runner graph and γ′ = γ. 191 is already running for 16 runners; its 17-door comes free; 239 is already in the table's n = 17 spectral list. (4) IDEA — the kill. The L7 analogue lives at level 289: a free coordinate blocks exactly 34 of 289 lifts (bad σ ∈ [0,17) ∪ [272,288]), so f ≤ 7 covers ≤ 238 < 289, and within each level-17p class a free coordinate blocks exactly 2 of 17 lifts. That is a per-core window-covering computation (13 windows of fraction 2/17, total 26/17 — overlapping, so genuinely decidable only by computation), laptop-cheap.
