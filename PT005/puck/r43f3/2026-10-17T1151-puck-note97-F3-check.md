## 1. Method (own code; Opus r43chk and Hesper tree3/scan code were NOT used as the engine)
- Engine: `check/puck_f3_check.py` (new numpy code, sha256 prefix 081ef2824c77d3a9). Definitions come from R43 COMMON (send_R43_GLM.txt):
  - open = improper (all T in 1..M/2 covered, 16·‖T·wᵢ‖_M < M, and ≥ 2 odd wᵢ) and not L7-closed (every even D | M checked).
  - Children = all 2¹⁵ lifts wᵢ or wᵢ + M at 2M, **deduplicated as multisets**, kept if open at 2M.
  - Fast child test: if the parent is improper, even T at 2M are inherited, so only odd T ≤ M are tested, using a packed-bit meet-in-the-middle over 2⁷ × 2⁸ lift halves. Then the parity filter, multiset dedup with `np.unique`, and a vectorised L7 check over every even D | 2M.
  - E = #{v₂(wᵢ) < k}, F3/F4/F5 = #{k − v₂(wᵢ) ≥ 3/4/5} at M = p·2ᵏ.
- Self-test: the fast child test agrees with the literal slow definitions on 960 of 960 random lifts and children (`SELFTEST_agree 960 of 960`). Every start node is checked open with the literal definitions.
- Cross-check: at every level, the per-level counts after ± canonicalisation equal the old Puck and Hesper logs (p47 336/3598/36/8, p53 167/1444/858/112, p59 412/928, p61 152/1292). In these trees, multiset dedup and ± dedup give the same counts.
- "Height" of a node = the number of further doublings in which it still has an open descendant (computed bottom-up over parent→child edges, with multiset ids).
- Second verification of the witnesses: `check/verify_witness_r43check.py` runs the functions of Puck's earlier pure-Python R43 checker (puck_r43_check.py) on the full root→16p paths: lift check, coverage of every T, odd count, and the full L7 table.
- Scan nodes: from Hesper's l30b/scan/*.json I read the **vectors only** (140). Puck's own 422 scan nodes (myscan.py output, not committed before) are copied into `check/puck_scan_nodes/`.
- Runtime: about 54 s for the main run (single process). The untracked files under PT005/puck/proofmap/3/realtree/ were **not used** and are not added.
