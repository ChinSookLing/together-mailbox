THE IDEA — "Fold the gate, then claim the census is shape-blind"

0. First, the one-line answer to the chair's "Why does every speed draw a shifted copy of the same shape?" (FACT). Fix a primitive root g mod p and write every time class a and speed class v in log coordinates. Then v·a covers time a iff va mod p lies in the ball B_p, and log_g(va) = log_g v + log_g a. Multiplication is addition in log space; therefore the set of time classes covered by v is exactly a translate of the fixed set D_p = log_g(B_p). The picture the table drew is the group law, not a coincidence.

1. The fold (FACT). The ball is symmetric: r ∈ B_p ⟺ −r ∈ B_p, and log_g(−r) = log_g r + (p−1)/2. Hence D_p is invariant under shift by (p−1)/2: it is a doubled copy of a smaller shape D̄_p on the quotient Z_{(p−1)/2}, with |D̄_p| = h := ⌊p/16⌋. A set of speed classes covers all time classes iff its reduction covers Z_{(p−1)/2} by translates of D̄_p. So the entire gate at p is one cyclic set-cover: it closes iff the covering number of D̄_p is ≥ 16. Everything else — families, rows, cores — is an unfolding of this single object.
