**Step 2 (arc count).** Write r = α + pσ, σ ∈ [0, 2^d); r odd ⟺ σ ≡ 1−α (mod 2). The arc {r : 16‖r‖_N < N} = [0, τ) ∪ (N−τ, N), τ = 2^{d−4}p ≥ 2p.
